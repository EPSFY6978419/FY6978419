# **Powering Spacecraft: How Advanced Battery Technology Could Transform Small Satellites**
---
### Abstract: {#abs}
Current small satellites still rely on high-energy lithium-ion batteries, the same batteries used in consumer electronics. lithium ions has to travel through a liquid electrolyte when charging and discharing [(Finkelstein et al., 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4cp00818a), this is inifficient and reduces the amount of charge a battery could store. This article would discuss how potentually using new approches to powering small satellites using Solid State, Sodium-Ion and other advanced batteries could change how small satellites are made. ...
